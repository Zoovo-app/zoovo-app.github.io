Rolling out across the Netherlands

Trusted local care, booked in a few taps.

Dog walking, pet sitting, boarding, doggy day care, cat sitting and puppy visits. Find a pro near you on a list or a map.

  1. Find

    Compare pros nearby. Badges show who's Highly rated, Reliable, Experienced and ID verified.

  2. Book and pay

    Pick a time and pay in the app with card, iDEAL or Apple Pay, securely through Stripe.

  3. Change of plans?

    You see the exact date and time your free cancellation ends. After that, the service's policy decides the refund.

  4. Review

    After the booking you review each other. Reviews show once you've both written one, or after 14 days.

How booking, payments and cancellations work

Safe for pets, fair for people

ID checked by Stripe

Every pro is 18 or older and has their identity checked by Stripe, our payment partner.

Checked by our team

Every service, with its photos and videos, is checked by the Zoovo team before it goes live.

Honest reviews

Only real, paid bookings can be reviewed. Reviews stay hidden until both sides have written one, so nobody writes in reply.

Help within 48 hours

Something went wrong? Report it from the start of the booking until 48 hours after it ends, and our team steps in.
