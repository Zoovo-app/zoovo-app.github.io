Rolling out across the Netherlands

Turn your love for animals into bookings.

Offer dog walking, pet sitting, boarding and more to pet parents near you. Zoovo takes care of bookings, payments and payouts.

Getting started

From the app to your first booking

  1. Get the app

    Download Zoovo, open the settings on your profile and tap Become a Service Provider.

  2. Verify with Stripe

    A photo of your ID and a selfie, plus your bank account, and your KvK details if you're a business. You need to be 18 or older.

  3. Create your service

    Add your own photos and videos, set your price and pick a cancellation policy: Flexible, Moderate or Strict.

  4. We check it

    Our team checks every service, usually within one business day. You get a notification and an email when it's live.

Getting paid

Your money, 48 hours after each booking

Pet parents pay in the app when they book, so you never chase a payment. Zoovo keeps 20% of the price and you receive the rest.

We send each booking's money 48 hours after it ends, unless a problem was reported. Stripe then pays it into your own bank account.

Your dashboard

Everything in one place

  • Today's and upcoming bookings, and a calendar.
  • Earnings: what's on its way and what's paid out this month.
  • Your services, stats and reviews.
  • Vacation mode, to pause new bookings while you're away.

Reviews, replies and badges

Pet parents review you after each booking, and you can answer each review with one public reply.

Badges like Highly rated, Reliable and Experienced come automatically from real bookings and reviews. Nobody can buy them.

Until you have 3 reviews, pet parents see NEW on your cards, and new pros get a small boost in their first month.

Promote a service

Share one of your services as a post. It shows in the Pros category of the feed, and in For you to pet parents near your service area.

You can promote each service once every 30 days. Promoting doesn't change search results: no one can pay to appear higher.
