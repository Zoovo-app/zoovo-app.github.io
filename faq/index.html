About Zoovo

What is Zoovo?

A pet app made in the Netherlands. Share your pet's life, meet pet parents nearby, plan playdates and book trusted local care, all in one place.

How much does Zoovo cost?

The app is free. You only pay for services you book, and you see the full price before you do.

Is Zoovo on Android?

Not yet. Zoovo is available for iPhone on the App Store.

Which languages does Zoovo speak?

English and Dutch. The app follows your phone's language and uses English when a language isn't translated yet.

Who can use Zoovo?

Anyone aged 13 and over. To offer services as a pro, you need to be 18 or older.

Booking care

Where can I book services?

Services are rolling out across the Netherlands. Open the app to see the pros near you.

How do I pay?

In the app, when you book, with card, iDEAL or Apple Pay. Payments are handled by Stripe, our payment partner.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Each service shows its cancellation policy before you book, and your booking shows the exact date and time free cancellation ends. After that you get 50% back, or nothing with a Strict policy.

Refunds go back to the way you paid and usually take a few days. If the pro cancels, you get everything back. See all policies.

What if something goes wrong?

Report a problem from the booking in the app, from the start until 48 hours after it ends. We hold the pro's payout while our team looks into it.

How do reviews work?

After a booking, you and the pro review each other. Reviews show once you've both written one, or after 14 days.

Your review of a pro is public. What a pro writes about you is only visible to you and that pro; other pros only see your average rating.

What do the badges mean?

Highly rated: 4.7 or more from at least 5 reviews. Reliable: no cancellations or no-shows by the pro in 90 days, and at least 5 completed bookings. Experienced: 3+ years' experience or at least 20 completed bookings. ID verified: identity checked by Stripe.

Badges are given automatically. Pros can't buy them.

For pros

Who can become a service provider?

Anyone aged 18 or over who verifies their identity and bank account with Stripe, plus their KvK details if they're a business. Becoming a service provider is rolling out across the Netherlands.

What does it cost to offer services?

Listing a service is free. When you're booked, Zoovo keeps 20% of the price and you receive the rest.

When do I get paid?

We send each booking's money 48 hours after it ends, unless a problem was reported. Stripe then pays it into your own bank account on its payout schedule.

How long does the check of my service take?

Usually one business day. You get a notification and an email when your service is live.

Can I promote my service?

Yes, once every 30 days per service, as a post in the feed. It doesn't change search results: no one can pay to appear higher.

Your account

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to your profile, open the settings and choose Delete account.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Thank you! Use the bug report form, or Report a bug in the app's settings.

How do I contact you?

Email info@zoovo.eu. We're Zoovo B.V., in the Netherlands.
