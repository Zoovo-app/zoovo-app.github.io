How booking, payments and cancellations work

Everything from finding a pro to the review afterwards, with no small print.

1. Find the right pro

Search for a service near you, then browse on a list or a map: dog walking, pet sitting, boarding, doggy day care, cat sitting or puppy visits.

Best match sorts services by their reviews, how close they are, how quickly the pro answers requests and how many bookings they've completed. New pros get a small boost in their first month. No one can pay to appear higher.

Highly rated

Rated 4.7 or more, from at least 5 reviews.

Reliable

No cancellations or no-shows by the pro in the last 90 days, and at least 5 completed bookings.

Experienced

3 or more years' experience, or at least 20 completed bookings on Zoovo.

ID verified

Identity checked by Stripe, our payment partner.

Badges are given automatically, from real bookings and reviews. Pros can't buy them. A pro shows NEW until they have 3 reviews.

2. Book and pay

Pick a time and pay in the app with card, iDEAL or Apple Pay. The price you see is the price you pay.

Payments are handled by Stripe, our payment partner, so your card details go straight to Stripe. The pro is only paid 48 hours after the booking ends.

3. Cancelling

Every service has one of three cancellation policies, shown before you book. Once you've booked, the app shows the exact date and time your free cancellation ends.

  • Refunds go back to the way you paid and usually take a few days.
  • If the pro cancels, you get a full refund.
  • If someone books the same time just before your payment comes through, you get a full refund automatically.

4. If something goes wrong

You can report a problem from the start of the booking until 48 hours after it ends, straight from the booking in the app.

We hold the pro's payout while our team looks into it, and we'll get back to you. That's why pros are paid 48 hours after a booking ends: there's time to sort things out first.

5. Review each other

After the booking, you and the pro can review each other. A review shows once you've both written one, or after 14 days, so nobody writes in reply to the other.

Reviews of pros are public and marked Booked and paid on Zoovo. Pros can answer each review with one public reply.

What a pro writes about you is only visible to you and that pro. Other pros only see your average rating.
