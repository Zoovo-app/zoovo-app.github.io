Safe for pets, fair for people

How we check pros and services, keep reviews honest and step in when something goes wrong.

Every pro is ID-checked

To offer services, pros must be 18 or older and verify their identity with Stripe, our payment partner: a photo of their ID and a selfie.

Stripe also checks their bank account, and their KvK details if they're a business. That's what the ID verified badge stands for.

Every service is checked by our team

Before a service goes live, the Zoovo team checks it, including every photo and video. When a pro changes a live service, we check the changes too.

Pros whose service breaks our rules can have it taken down, and their professional profile suspended.

Reviews you can trust

Only bookings made and paid on Zoovo can be reviewed, and those reviews say Booked and paid on Zoovo.

Reviews stay hidden until both sides have written one, or for 14 days, so nobody writes in reply. Pros get one public reply per review, which they can't change later.

Badges come automatically from real bookings and reviews. Nobody can buy them, and no one can pay to appear higher in search.

Payments, held until the job is done

You pay in the app, through Stripe. The pro is paid 48 hours after the booking ends, so there's time to report a problem first.

If you report one, we hold the payout while our team looks into it.

Report, block, and we follow up

  • Report a problem with a booking from its start until 48 hours after it ends.
  • Report a service, a review, a reply or a post that doesn't look right.
  • Block anyone you don't want to hear from.

Our team looks at every report. We can hide reviews and replies, take services down and suspend professional profiles.

Age

Zoovo is for people aged 13 and over. Professional accounts are for adults (18+).

Worried about something?

Email us at info@zoovo.eu. If a person or animal is in danger, call 112 first.
