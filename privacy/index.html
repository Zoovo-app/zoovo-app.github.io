Zoovo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-07

This Privacy Policy explains what information Zoovo collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. Zoovo is a social network for pets and their people. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Data controller: Zoovo B.V. (Netherlands)

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at info@zoovo.eu.

1. Who we are

Zoovo is a mobile application that lets pet owners create profiles for themselves and their pets, share posts (photos and videos), comment, send direct messages, and discover other pet owners nearby. The app is built on Flutter and uses Supabase for our backend, database, authentication, and file storage.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information we need to run the service.

Information you give us

  • Account details — your email address and password (passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we never see your plain-text password).
  • Profile information — your name, city, and profile photos.
  • Pets — details about your pets, such as their name, species, photos, and other information you choose to add.
  • Content you create — posts, photos, videos, captions, and comments.
  • Direct messages — the content of messages you send to and receive from other users.

Information we collect automatically

  • Approximate location — to help you find pet owners and content nearby, we use a coarse location expressed only as approximate distance bands (for example, "within 5 km"). Your exact location is never shared with other users — only coarse distance bands are shown. See Section 5 for details.
  • Device push tokens — if you enable notifications, we store a device token so we can deliver push notifications to you. Notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service.
  • App usage and diagnostics — we use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, for example which screens are opened and when posts, playdates, or messages are created. This includes an app-instance identifier, your device model and operating system, and the actions you take in the app — never the content of your posts or messages. We don't show ads, and we don't use this data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

  • create and maintain your account;
  • show your profile, pets, posts, and comments to other users as you direct;
  • deliver direct messages between you and other users;
  • show you relevant nearby content and people using coarse distance bands;
  • send push notifications you have opted into;
  • understand how the app is used so we can fix problems and improve it;
  • keep Zoovo safe — including moderating content, responding to reports, and removing objectionable content or abusive users (see our Terms of Service);
  • comply with our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where your data is stored

Your account data, profile, pets, posts, comments, and messages are stored in our Supabase project database. Photos and videos you upload are kept in Supabase image and file storage. Data may be processed on servers operated by our infrastructure providers. We apply access controls so that data is only accessible as needed to operate the service.

5. Location and how it is shared

Zoovo is location-aware so you can connect with nearby pet owners. To protect your privacy:

  • We convert your location into coarse distance bands before it is shown to anyone.
  • We never share your precise GPS coordinates or exact address with other users.
  • Other users only ever see an approximate distance (a band), never a pinpoint.

6. Sharing your information

Other Zoovo users can see the content you choose to make visible — your profile, pets, posts, comments, and the approximate distance bands described above. Direct messages are visible only to you and the people in the conversation.

We may share information with service providers strictly to operate the app — Supabase (backend, database, authentication, and file storage) and Google Firebase (usage analytics and push notification delivery) — and where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account (see Section 8), we delete your account and the associated data — including your profile, pets, posts, comments, and messages — except where we are required to retain certain records to comply with the law or to resolve disputes. Backups are purged on a rolling schedule.

8. Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time from inside the app:

Profile → Settings → Delete account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your account, pets, posts, comments, and messages. This action cannot be undone.

9. Children

Zoovo is not intended for children under 13. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account and use the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@zoovo.eu.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can update most of your information directly in the app, delete your account as described above, or contact us at info@zoovo.eu to make a request.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including access controls and encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

13. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:

info@zoovo.eu
